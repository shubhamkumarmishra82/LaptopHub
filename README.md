 <p>LaptopHub – Full Stack Laptop E-Commerce Website</p>

LaptopHub is a full-stack laptop e-commerce web application developed using Java Spring Boot, React.js, Redux Toolkit, and MySQL.

The application allows users to explore laptops, search products, add items to their cart, place orders, manage their profiles, and submit reviews. It also provides an admin panel to manage products, orders, users, and customer reviews.

 <p> 🚀 Features</p>

<p> 👤  User Features</p>
User Registration and Login , JWT-based Authentication , Browse All Available Laptops , Search and Filter Products
View Detailed Product Information , Add Products to Shopping Cart , Increase and Decrease Cart Quantity , Buy Now Functionality
Place Orders and Checkout , View My Orders , Cancel Orders , View and Update My Profile , Change Password , Submit, Update, and Delete Reviews

 <p> 🔐 Admin Features</p>
Admin Registration and Login , Admin Dashboard
View Total Products, Orders, Users, and Reviews
Add New Products , Update Product Details , Delete Products
Manage Customer Orders , Update Order Status
Manage Payment Status
View Registered Users
Approve or Reject Customer Reviews

<p> 🛒 E-Commerce Features</p>
Dynamic Product Listing , Product Details Page
Shopping Cart Management , Buy Now and Cart Checkout
Order Management , Customer Testimonials
